Internal-use terms

Updated 26 October 2026. NATIV gbrain Drive Backup is an internal utility for the authorized company account, not a public commercial backup service.

Operation is limited to the company-approved backup and recovery workflow. An operator must authorize Google access and independently keep the backup encryption recovery credential. Google Drive storage limits and the company's separate GitHub, Bitwarden, and source-database arrangements continue to apply.

A stored backup is considered verified only after the corresponding recovery checks succeed. Availability, completeness, or recovery speed is not guaranteed merely by the presence of an uploaded file. Revoking Google authorization prevents future access but does not remove existing backup objects.

Changes to access, retention, expenditure, or backup destinations require the company's applicable authorization. Contact: info@mm-ai.co.
